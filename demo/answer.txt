● The deploy fails because the migration never releases its lock.

  What happens, in order:

  1. The release job starts the migration and takes a table lock.
  2. The old pods are still serving traffic, and their health
     checks keep a long transaction open on the same table.
  3. The migration waits for that transaction, hits the 5 minute
     job timeout, and the rollout is marked failed.

  Two ways out:

  a. Drain the old pods before migrating. Safest, but it adds
     about two minutes of reduced capacity to every deploy.
  b. Move the migration to a pre-deploy hook with lock_timeout
     set, so it fails fast and retries instead of hanging.

  I'd go with (b). Want me to write the hook?
